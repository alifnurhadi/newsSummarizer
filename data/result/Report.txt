**Key Regulatory Considerations for Investors**

In our advisory, we emphasize three critical regulatory constraints that investors must consider when navigating Indonesia's capital markets:

1. **FCPA Compliance**: Indonesian listed companies engaging in dual listing on foreign stock exchanges, such as the US, must comply with Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) regulations to prevent corruption. This requires ensuring all transactions and interactions with government officials are transparent, ethical, and compliant with anti-corruption laws.
2. **Independent Legal and Compliance Director**: Listed companies must establish an independent legal and compliance director responsible for overseeing corporate governance, business integrity, and audit compliance. This ensures the company has a dedicated expert to monitor and enforce regulatory requirements.
3. **Chief Integrity Officer (CIO)**: Companies must appoint a CIO who reports directly to the Board of Commissioners to ensure corporate governance, business integrity, and audit compliance. The CIO plays a crucial role in promoting transparency, accountability, and good corporate governance practices within the organization.
